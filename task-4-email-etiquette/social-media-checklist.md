#  Responsible Social Media Use – Checklist

> Stay safe, respectful, and responsible while using social media.

---

## ✅ Do’s

- 🧠 **Think before you post** — ensure your content is appropriate  
- 🔐 **Use strong passwords** — keep your accounts secure  
- 🔍 **Verify information** — avoid spreading misinformation  
- 💼 **Maintain professionalism** — build a positive digital identity  
- 🙏 **Respect privacy** — take consent before posting about others  

---

## ❌ Don’ts

- 🚫 **Don’t share personal information** — protect your privacy  
- ⚠️ **Avoid offensive content** — be respectful to everyone  
- 💬 **Don’t engage in cyberbullying** — promote a healthy environment  
- 👤 **Avoid unknown requests** — stay cautious online  
- ⏳ **Don’t overuse social media** — maintain a healthy balance
