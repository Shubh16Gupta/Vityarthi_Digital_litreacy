#  Responsible Social Media Use –Checklist

> Stay safe,respectful,and responsible while using social media

---

## Do’s

- ensure your content is appropriate  
- keep your accounts secure  
- avoid spreading misinformation  
- build a positive digital identity  
- take consent before posting about others  

---

## Don’ts

- protect your privacy  
- be respectful to everyone  
- promote a healthy environment  
- stay cautious online  
- maintain a healthy balance
