Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

General Safety Tip
1. Use strong and unique passwords for all your online accounts
2. Enable two-factor authentication for added security.
3. Always verrify links and mail before clicking to avoid fake websites.
4. Do not share personal information such as passwords, OTPs, or ID details
5. Avoid accepting friend requests or messages from unknown people
6. Always log out when using public or shared devices.
7. Never share your upi pin with anyone, even if they claim to be from the bank.
8. Carefully check payment requests before approving any transaction
9. Do not scan unknown qr codes or click on suspicious payment links.

- National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
- Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

Conclusion
Following these simple precautions can help prevent cybercrime and ensure a safer online experience. Awareness and responsible digital behavior are key to protecting personal and financial information.
